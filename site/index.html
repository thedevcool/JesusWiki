JesusWiki

What the Bible records about Jesus, set beside sources written outside the Bible. Each claim is graded by how strong the evidence is, and counterarguments are part of the entry. Anyone can contribute.

How claims are graded

Widely agreed
Independent sources, and most historians accept it, including non-Christian scholars.
Probable
Good evidence from at least one outside source, with some open questions.
Debated
Scholars disagree about the source or what it shows.
Weak
The source is lost, late, or does not actually mention Jesus. Kept here so nobody overclaims it.

Add to the wiki

Anyone can propose an entry. The quickest way is to fill in the entry form. If you are comfortable with GitHub, add a JSON file to the entries folder and open a pull request. The contributor guide has the format and the rules.

Ground rules: cite a real, checkable source. Summarize in your own words rather than pasting long passages. Say when a source was hostile to Christians, since that strengthens it. Say when a source may have been edited later or depends on Christian reports. Every entry includes the strongest objection. Entries that overclaim are graded down, not deleted.
